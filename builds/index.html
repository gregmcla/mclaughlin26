Builds

About 50 repositories since January 2025 to October 2026. Four are written up in full. The rest are here because range is part of the point, and some of them are just fun.

Written up in full

  • MicroCapRebuilderA simulated multi-portfolio decision system with an AI portfolio manager that has to show its work. The longest-running build, and the one with the most honest evaluation.
  • Buyer Universe GeneratorSell-side buyer scoring and confidential outreach drafting for a deal firm. No auto-send, ever.
  • DealCloud connector and DebriefAsk the CRM a question in plain language. Paste notes, get a logged record. Ten people used it unprompted.
  • AI operating system for a deal firmTwelve use-case blueprints across the sell-side lifecycle and a ninety-day plan. The strategist half.

More deal-firm tooling

  • Deal intelligence dashboardLocation-level performance, payor mix, valuation sensitivity grid, comparable transactions, and a CIM narrative builder for a live engagement. Next.js, PostgreSQL.
  • QoE ScreenerUpload a financial databook, get a quality-of-earnings screen: key metrics, risk flags, a written narrative, and a recommendation on whether a formal engagement is warranted. Replaced an Excel model.
  • Exit RadarFinds business owners likely thinking about selling before they start shopping for advisors, from the owner side and the buyer side.
  • ReferralKitTurns one referral-partner meeting into activation assets: a follow-up, a forwardable intro, a referral trigger map, a CRM note, and a forwardability score.
  • Partner dashboardReplaced an account-map workbook. Top referral partners, touches, ninety-day plans. Built on the idea "map the partner, not the clients."
  • The Deal TapeScans the news firehose for capital in motion, writes the deal read for owners and acquirers, renders a newspaper-style page.
  • Claude skills for deal workMeeting prep that researches a person and a firm. Branded pitch materials. Industry M&A one-pagers. Email drafting in the firm's voice.

AI systems and research

  • Intake OSAn operator console for a document-intake review agent on synthetic data: proposals, validation, explicit human decisions, and an approval-only ledger, with an evaluation board.
  • RoscoA local-first marketing intelligence app that runs entirely in the browser on about 83,000 synthetic records, with a parity document stating what works and what does not.
  • The Morning SignalA daily intelligence broadsheet. Claude pulls the day's stories, synthesizes what is emerging that nobody has built yet, renders a single-file newspaper on a schedule.
  • ai-sessionsA dashboard for running several Claude Code and Codex sessions at once, with live tool-call streaming and prompt dispatch to idle sessions.
  • Fantasy football assistantRun deliberately the way a deployment strategist runs a customer project: baseline first, then news checks, then a recommendation log. For a private league. Taken very seriously.

  • villain.meSix questions in, a comic-book supervillain origin story out.
  • Landing pagesA dozen of them, for concepts from a part-time AI department for contractors to a fire-safety compliance service to a one-of-one baseball glove. Each a sandbox for a design system and a voice.
